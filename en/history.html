Spiritual Heritage

Our
History

A journey of unwavering faith connecting apostolic origins with nationwide expansion in Panama.

Eternal Origin 1st Century

Day of Pentecost

The origin of the Christian church is marked by the descent of the Holy Spirit in Jerusalem. This historic and spiritual event established the apostolic foundation that IPUL preserves to this day.

"And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues..." — Acts 2:4
1964 — 1990 Stage I

Sowing at the frontier

"God opens a path through the jungle"

In this first stage, the work was born in secrecy, far from the cities, deep in the Darien. There were no structures, no resources, no paved roads — only a divine calling and hearts willing to obey.

God began writing this story through pioneers like Gabriel Aji, Anibal Cansari, and Ember Ochoa — humble men with steadfast faith who received the gospel message and spread it among indigenous and rural communities.

The jungle witnessed the first altars, raised amid limitations but filled with the presence of God.

This stage was marked by

Evangelism under adverse conditions

Conversion of entire families

Formation of faith communities without formal structure

There was no organization yet, but there was a living work. It was the time when the seed was planted with tears, effort, and radical obedience.

Stage II 1991 — 1998

Time of the calling

"God opens doors in the city"

With the arrival of missionaries Orlando Jimenez and his wife Teresa, a new stage begins: the transition from the jungle to the city. God begins to order, structure, and give direction to the work.

Later, missionaries Fernando Sierra and his wife Claribel Calvo join, strengthening the vision of national expansion.

This stage represents

Establishment in Panama City

Initial organization of the church

Opening new congregations in urban and coastal areas

God opens strategic doors and positions the work in key locations. The church begins to be recognized and grows with direction.

1999 — 2010 Stage III

Time of multiplication

"God extends the work"

Leadership & Expansion

Esdras Barranco and Elsa Hurtado

Under their leadership, IPUL Panama enters a stage of significant expansion. The work ceases to be just a growing movement and becomes a network of churches across different regions of the country.

During this stage we see

Multiplication of congregations

Opening new works in provinces and rural areas

The missionary vision is strengthened and begins to be embraced by the local church. It is no longer just sowing — now it multiplies.

Stage IV 2011 — 2024

Time of consolidation

"God establishes the foundation"

2011 — 2016

Japhet Masmela and Patricia Suarez

The work enters a process of spiritual and organizational strengthening.

Strengthened areas

Ministerial structure

Doctrinal teaching

Holistic discipleship

2017 — 2023

Jose Martinez and Mirna Parra

The church took another step forward toward consolidation.

Key emphases

Ministry to the nation's children

Strengthening pastoral leadership

In this stage the church affirms itself, stabilizes, and prepares for new challenges. What was sown and multiplied is now firmly established.

Phase V 2024 — onward

Time of Projection

"The mission advances"

Guillermo and Janeth Rativa

With their arrival, a new phase begins: the projection of missions in Panama.

The church enters a time where

Strengthened areas la vision misionera nacional

Administrative structure is implemented

Training and development spaces are expanded and strengthened across all areas of church growth

This stage is oriented toward growth and consolidation of institutional identity, reaffirming the profile and role of ministers and pastors' wives, as well as the function of each official role.
