Apostolic faith in Panama

A faith with biblical roots and life for today.

At IPUL Panama, we believe that the Bible guides faith and life. We are a Pentecostal Christian church seeking to live the gospel of Jesus Christ with clarity, hope, and service to our nation.

Our conviction

God's Word stands firm and calls us to know Jesus Christ, grow in community, and serve with love.

Our foundation

The Bible guides our faith and conduct.

As the United Pentecostal Church of Latin America in Panama, we receive the Holy Scriptures as the only authority given by God for doctrine, faith, hope, and the instruction of the Church. We seek to read and study them under the direction of the Holy Spirit.

Apostolic faith is not a museum piece. It invites us into a living relationship with Jesus Christ and a responsible response to the needs of our families, neighborhoods, and cities.

This page summarizes only the Articles of Faith in the IPUL Manual approved by the General Assembly of Ministers; it does not replace them.

What we believe

Three convictions that shape our life.

We express our faith with humility, seeking for every conviction to be reflected in a life of obedience, compassion, and hope.

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Colossians 2:9

One God, revealed in Jesus Christ

We believe in the one living, eternal, and indivisible God. In Jesus Christ we recognize true God and true man; in Him the whole fullness of the Deity dwells bodily.

Isaiah 55:7 · Acts 2:38

Conversion and new birth

We proclaim genuine repentance, baptism by immersion in the Name of Jesus Christ, and the infilling of the Holy Spirit as the apostolic response to the gospel.

1 Peter 1:15–16 · Hebrews 12:14

Holy living

We understand holiness as a life set apart for God: a visible way to love our neighbor, act with integrity, and reflect Christ every day.

Life in the Spirit

The Spirit equips us and sustains us in hope.

These articles of faith guide our worship, service, and expectation of the return of Jesus Christ.

Acts 2:4 · 1 Corinthians 12:7

Spirit baptism and gifts

We believe that baptism in the Holy Spirit is initially manifested by speaking in other tongues. Its gifts are for the benefit and edification of the Church and the spread of the gospel, not for personal exaltation.

James 5:14–16 · 1 Corinthians 11:23–29

Prayer, healing, and communion

We pray in faith for those who need healing and celebrate the breaking of bread in remembrance of Christ, as an expression of fellowship and the unity of His body.

1 Thessalonians 4:13–17 · Revelation 1:7

Future hope

We await the resurrection, the gathering of the Church before the great tribulation, and the personal second coming of Jesus Christ. We trust in His kingdom and His righteous final judgment.

A faith that serves Panama

The gospel becomes close in our nation.

Through our congregations, we seek to accompany people through prayer, biblical teaching, and service. Our Panamanian identity is expressed in a church that welcomes, listens, and works for the well-being of its communities.

  • We worship God with gratitude and proclaim Jesus Christ with respect.
  • We equip families, children, youth, and leaders through biblical teaching.
  • We serve responsibly in every province where IPUL Panama is present.
  • We value the unity of the church and the dignity of every person.

Worldwide fellowship

A fellowship beyond borders.

IPUL Panama is part of the Pentecostal International Fellowship, a global community of apostolic Pentecostal churches united in faith in Jesus Christ. This fellowship strengthens our apostolic identity and our vision to bring the gospel to every nation.

Frequently asked questions

Get to know our faith.

What kind of church is IPUL Panama?

We are an apostolic Pentecostal Christian church, part of the United Pentecostal Church of Latin America. We believe the Bible is the rule of faith and conduct for Christian life.

What does apostolic doctrine mean?

It is teaching based on the message preached by the apostles, expressed in repentance, baptism in the Name of Jesus Christ, and the infilling of the Holy Spirit.

Can I learn more or visit a congregation?

Yes. We invite you to find a nearby location, attend a service, and speak with our community. There is room for your questions and your faith journey.

We are here to walk with you if you would like to know more about Jesus Christ and church life in Panama.
